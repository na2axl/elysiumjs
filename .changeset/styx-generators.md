The generators name files and module folders in kebab-case (`user-profile.service.ts`). `make:validator` writes a schema-only validator and adds `authorize()` and `validate()` only with `--authorize` and `--validate`. `make:model` declares `supportTenancy` only for a tenant-scoped model (`--support-tenancy`).
